Our Story

Habesha Eatery brings the rich flavors of Ethiopian cuisine to Bole, Addis Ababa. Our restaurant celebrates traditional recipes, fresh ingredients, and the warmth of Ethiopian hospitality.

From our famous doro wat to delicious vegetarian dishes,every meal is prepared with care to give our guests an authentic dining experience.
